TapDosh Refund Policy

Effective date: April 6, 2026

TapDosh may review and approve refund requests for eligible orders placed through the platform or its supported payment and WhatsApp ordering flow.

Customers may be eligible for a refund if

  • The order was not delivered.
  • The order was incorrect or incomplete.
  • The order was cancelled before preparation.

Refund methods

  • Refund to the original payment method, including Paystack where applicable.
  • TapDosh wallet credit or equivalent order credit where offered.

Processing time

  • Approved refunds are typically processed within 24 to 72 hours.

For dispute handling or refund support, contact TapDosh through WhatsApp with your order details and issue summary.
