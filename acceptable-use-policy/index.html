TapDosh Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: April 6, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy explains the types of listings, activities, and conduct allowed on TapDosh. Vendors and users must comply with this policy when using the TapDosh platform and WhatsApp ordering flow.

Vendors must not list or sell

  • Illegal goods or services.
  • Drugs or controlled substances.
  • Weapons or harmful items.
  • Fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading products.
  • Copyright-infringing content or unauthorized branded items.

Vendor obligations

  • Provide accurate menu listings, pricing, and item descriptions.
  • Fulfill confirmed orders promptly and professionally.
  • Maintain proper food safety, hygiene, and handling standards.

TapDosh rights

  • TapDosh may suspend, restrict, or remove vendors or listings that breach this policy.
  • TapDosh may withhold payouts or pause order handling where fraud, abuse, or suspicious activity is identified.
  • TapDosh may report illegal activity or misuse to relevant authorities where required.

Questions about this policy can be directed through TapDosh support on WhatsApp during operating hours.
